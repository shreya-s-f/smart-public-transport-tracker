How accurate is the Live GPS Bus Tracking?

Live buses stream telemetry updates every 10 seconds. Real road curvatures and turns are calculated via the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) engine over OpenStreetMap data.

Is Bagalkot transit supported on the live map?

Yes! Bagalkot city shuttles (Navanagar to College Cross, Bagalkot Railway Station, Vidyagiri, BVVS Campus) are fully indexed with real street turns and verified stop coordinates.

How do I show my digital e-ticket to the conductor?

Simply tap any trip from your Recent Trips list to open the digital ticket pass or download a PDF pass to present offline.
